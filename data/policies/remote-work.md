# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Owner: People team · Effective: 1 March 2026 · Applies to: all employees after their probation period

## Hybrid work model

Employees work from the office at least 2 days per week. Team leads may set one fixed common office day per team. Client-site days count as office days. During the 3-month probation period, new joiners work from the office at least 4 days per week.

## Core hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time, Monday to Friday. During core hours you must be reachable on Teams and phone. Outside core hours, work time is flexible within the limits of the Labour Code and your engagement's needs.

## Home office allowance

Employees who work from home at least 1 day per week receive:

- a one-off home office equipment allowance of HUF 60,000, claimable once every 3 years for a chair, desk, monitor or headset, with receipts;
- a monthly internet and utility allowance of HUF 8,000, paid automatically with salary.

Laptops, docking stations and phones are provided by IT and are not covered by the allowance.

## Working from abroad

You may work remotely from another EU or EEA country, Switzerland or the United Kingdom for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Each stay needs approval from your line manager and the People team at least 10 working days in advance, because of tax and social-security rules. Working remotely from any other country is not allowed. Business trips do not count towards the 20 days. Remote work abroad is not a business trip, so no meal allowance or travel costs are paid.

## Security when working remotely

Follow the IT Security Policy wherever you work. Never work on client documents in public places where the screen can be seen, and always connect through the firm's VPN on networks you do not control.
