# Onboarding Guide

Owner: People team · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Applies to: all new joiners

## Before your first day

You receive your firm email address and a welcome pack by email 5 working days before your start date. Bring a photo ID, your tax identification card and your social security (TAJ) card on your first day.

## Your first day

Arrive at the reception of the Budapest office at 09:00. The day includes collecting your laptop and access card from IT, a welcome session with the People team, and lunch with your onboarding buddy.

## Onboarding buddy

Every new joiner is assigned an onboarding buddy from their team for the first 3 months. Your buddy is your first contact for practical questions; your line manager remains responsible for your work and performance.

## Mandatory training

Complete these e-learning courses within 30 calendar days of your start date:

- Information security basics (about 45 minutes)
- Code of conduct and independence (about 60 minutes)
- Data protection and GDPR (about 40 minutes)

Access to client systems is granted only after all three are completed.

## Probation period

The probation period is 3 months. Your line manager holds a check-in with you after 6 weeks and a probation review before the end of the third month.
