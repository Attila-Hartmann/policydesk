# IT Security Policy

Owner: IT Security · Effective: 1 February 2026 · Applies to: all employees, contractors and interns

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused from any other service. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) with the Authenticator app is mandatory for all firm accounts. Never share your password or MFA codes, not even with IT support: IT will never ask for them.

## Approved devices and storage

Client and firm data may only be stored on firm-managed laptops and phones and in the firm's approved cloud storage (the firm's SharePoint and OneDrive). USB storage devices are blocked on all firm laptops. Personal devices may access email and Teams only through the managed mobile app.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop, phone or access card to the IT Service Desk (+36 1 555 0100, available 24/7) within 1 hour of noticing it. IT will wipe the device remotely. If the device was stolen, also file a police report and send the reference number to IT within 3 working days.

## Phishing and suspicious messages

Report suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button in Outlook, or forward them to security@pannonfield.example. Do not click links or open attachments in suspicious messages. If you clicked a link or entered your password, call the IT Service Desk immediately.

## Generative AI tools

Only the firm's approved internal AI assistant may be used with client or firm data. Public AI chatbots and translation sites may be used only with public information. Pasting client names, client documents, personal data or source code into public AI tools is a disciplinary matter.

## Software installation

Install software only from the Company Portal. Requests for other software go to the IT Service Desk and are reviewed by IT Security within 5 working days.
