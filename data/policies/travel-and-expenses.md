# Travel and Expenses Policy

Owner: Finance · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Applies to: all employees of Pannonfield Advisory Kft.

## Booking business travel

All business trips are booked through the Travel Desk (travel@pannonfield.example) at least 10 working days before departure. Trips booked later need written approval from your engagement manager. Personal travel agencies and private booking sites may only be used if the Travel Desk confirms the fare is at least 15% cheaper.

## Flights

Economy class is the standard for all flights. Business class is allowed only for flights with a scheduled duration of more than 6 hours, and only with prior approval from a partner. Low-cost carriers are acceptable for trips within Europe; one checked bag per direction is reimbursed.

## Rail and local transport

Rail travel is preferred over flying for destinations reachable within 4 hours by train (for example Vienna and Bratislava). First-class rail is reimbursed on journeys longer than 2 hours. Taxis and ride-hailing are reimbursed between 22:00 and 06:00, when carrying equipment, or when public transport is not available; otherwise use public transport.

## Accommodation

Hotel costs are reimbursed up to the following nightly caps, including taxes and breakfast:

- Budapest and other Hungarian cities: HUF 45,000
- Vienna, Prague, Bratislava, Warsaw: EUR 150
- London: GBP 220
- Any other city: EUR 170

Stays above the cap need prior approval from your engagement manager. Airbnb and similar rentals are allowed for stays of 5 nights or longer.

## Daily meal allowance

Instead of reimbursing individual meals, the firm pays a fixed daily meal allowance for each full day spent on a business trip abroad:

- Vienna: EUR 45
- Prague, Bratislava, Warsaw, Bucharest: EUR 35
- London: GBP 55
- Any other city abroad: EUR 40

On the day of departure and the day of return, half of the daily amount is paid. There is no meal allowance for trips within Hungary. If a client or the firm pays for a meal, the allowance for that day is reduced by 25% per meal.

## Using your own car

Using your own car for business trips needs prior approval. Mileage is reimbursed at HUF 120 per kilometre, plus motorway tolls and parking. Fines are never reimbursed.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in the Expense app within 30 calendar days of the end of the trip. Attach an itemised receipt for every item above HUF 5,000. Expenses submitted later than 60 days after the trip are not reimbursed, except with written approval from the Finance Director. Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly salary.
