# Data Protection Policy

Owner: Data Protection Officer · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Applies to: all employees and contractors

## Personal data we handle

The firm processes personal data of employees, client contacts and, during engagements, of the clients' own customers and employees. Treat all personal data as confidential and use it only for the purpose it was collected for.

## Data minimisation

Request and keep only the personal data an engagement needs. Anonymise or pseudonymise data before analysis wherever possible. Never copy client personal data into email bodies, chat messages or personal notes.

## Retention

Engagement files containing personal data are kept for 8 years after the end of the engagement, as required by Hungarian accounting and audit rules, and then deleted. Recruitment data of unsuccessful candidates is deleted after 6 months unless the candidate agrees to a longer period.

## Data breaches

A data breach is any loss, theft or unauthorised disclosure of personal data, including an email sent to the wrong recipient. Report every suspected breach to the Data Protection Officer (dpo@pannonfield.example) without delay and at most within 24 hours of noticing it. The firm must notify the Hungarian data protection authority (NAIH) within 72 hours where required, so do not wait until you are certain.

## Clean desk

Lock your screen (Windows key + L) whenever you leave your desk. Printed documents containing personal or client data must be locked away at the end of the day and disposed of in the secure shredding bins.
