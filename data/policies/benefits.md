# Benefits Overview

Owner: People team · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Applies to: all employees with a permanent contract

## Cafeteria allowance

Every employee receives a cafeteria allowance of HUF 450,000 per calendar year, which can be split between the SZÉP card, a private pension fund and a health fund. Choose your split in the HR portal by 15 January; new joiners choose within 30 days of their start date and receive a pro-rated amount.

## Health insurance

Private health insurance with the firm's partner clinic is provided from the first day of employment. It covers specialist consultations, diagnostic tests and an annual health check. Family members can be added at your own cost through payroll deduction.

## Employee assistance programme

The confidential employee assistance programme offers up to 6 free counselling sessions per topic per year, by phone, video or in person, for psychological, legal or financial questions. The firm never learns who used the service.

## Sports and wellbeing

The firm pays 50% of the monthly fee for one gym or sports pass, up to HUF 12,000 per month. Claims are made monthly in the Expense app with the invoice attached.

## Mobile phone

Employees in client-facing roles receive a firm mobile phone with unlimited domestic calls and 20 GB of EU roaming data per month. Private use is allowed within these limits.
