# Code of Conduct

Owner: Ethics and Compliance · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Applies to: all employees and partners

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept gifts from clients or suppliers worth up to HUF 15,000 per occasion, and no more than HUF 40,000 from the same source in a calendar year. Cash, vouchers and gift cards are never accepted, whatever their value. Register every gift above HUF 5,000 in the Gift Register within 5 working days. Hospitality such as business meals is acceptable if it is reasonable and has a clear business purpose.

## Conflicts of interest

Declare any personal relationship, financial interest or outside job that could influence your work, in the Compliance portal, before you start work on the affected engagement. Outside employment, paid board roles and paid teaching need prior written approval from Ethics and Compliance.

## Independence

Employees working on audit clients must not hold shares or other financial interests in those clients. Check the Restricted Entities List before buying any security. Breaches must be reported within 2 working days of discovery.

## Speaking up

Raise concerns about unethical or illegal behaviour with your line manager, with Ethics and Compliance (ethics@pannonfield.example), or anonymously through the Speak Up hotline (+36 1 555 0199). The firm does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Social media

You may say that you work at the firm, but never name clients, describe engagements, or post anything that could be read as the firm's official opinion without approval from the Communications team.
