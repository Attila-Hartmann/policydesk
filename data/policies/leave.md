# Leave Policy

Owner: People team · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Applies to: all employees

## Annual leave entitlement

Every employee receives 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees with 5 or more years of service at the firm receive 2 additional days. Joiners and leavers receive a pro-rated amount for the part of the year they are employed.

## Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR portal. Requests for more than 3 consecutive working days must be submitted at least 10 working days in advance. Leave during the busy season (15 January to 31 March) needs approval from your engagement manager as well as your line manager.

## Carrying leave over

Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year. Carried-over days must be taken by 31 March of the next year, or they are lost. Exceptions are only possible for long-term sickness or parental leave.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, notify your line manager and the People team (people@pannonfield.example) by 09:00 on the first day of absence. For absences longer than 2 working days, a medical certificate is required. The firm tops up statutory sick pay to 100% of base salary for up to 15 working days per year.

## Volunteering day

Every employee can take 1 paid volunteering day per year for work with a registered charity. It cannot be carried over.

## Parental leave

Fathers receive 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 2 months of the child's birth. The firm tops up the statutory benefit to 100% of base salary for these days. Maternity and parental leave follow the Labour Code; contact the People team for an individual plan.
