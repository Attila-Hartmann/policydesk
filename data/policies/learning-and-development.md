# Learning and Development Policy

Owner: People team · Effective: 1 January 2026 · Applies to: all employees after their probation period

## Annual training budget

Every employee has an individual training budget of HUF 400,000 per calendar year for external courses, conferences and books related to their role. Unused budget is not carried over. Spending above HUF 100,000 per item needs approval from your line manager before you book.

## Professional certifications

The firm pays the full exam fees for role-relevant certifications, for example ACCA, CFA, CISA and Microsoft Azure or AWS cloud certifications. Exam fees do not count against the annual training budget. The firm pays for each exam at most twice; a third attempt is at your own cost.

## Study leave

For certification exams you receive up to 5 working days of paid study leave per calendar year, in addition to annual leave. Request study leave in the HR portal at least 10 working days before the exam.

## Repayment agreement

If the firm pays more than HUF 1,000,000 in total for a single qualification programme, you sign a repayment agreement. If you leave the firm within 24 months of completing the programme, you repay the cost pro rata (for example, leaving after 12 months means repaying half).
